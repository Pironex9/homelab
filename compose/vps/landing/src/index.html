Komodo GitOps Migration

Twenty Docker Compose stacks moved off Dockge and onto Komodo without dropping a service. Every stack now lives in Git, and Komodo pulls and redeploys straight from the repository when a commit lands.

Read the write-up

Resilient Storage

Two internal and two USB drives pooled into one 8.1 TB MergerFS volume with SnapRAID parity on top. It survives a single drive failure with no data loss, and systemd timers run the sync and scrub unattended.

Read the write-up

Self-hosted Tunnel

Selected services reach the public internet through Pangolin on a Hetzner VPS, joined to the homelab by a WireGuard tunnel. No Cloudflare in the path and no ports forwarded on the home router.

Read the write-up

Backup System

Restic snapshots to a local disk and to an NFS share on a second machine, driven by a shell script on a schedule. Each source carries its own retention policy, and restores are tested rather than assumed.
